## Current-presentation assessment (Track W)

Desktop and mobile evidence was visually inspected from the two stable captures dated 2026-09-01. The desktop first view has an official shopfront image, logo, headline, generic value copy, a Call Us button and an award-like graphic. The mobile first view presents the logo and shopfront, then a cookie-consent layer before the main service/appointment proposition is readable.

This is not a claim that the business has no usable website. The site does communicate business, location, phone and appointment-only operation. The evidence-backed persuasion gap is that a prospective cat or dog owner cannot immediately see a deliberately ordered service choice, care / qualification proof, recent customer reassurance and one unobscured enquiry path in a coherent first-to-next-scroll journey. The current prominent visual materials are a shopfront and badge-like graphic; the service and proof hierarchy is fragmented.
