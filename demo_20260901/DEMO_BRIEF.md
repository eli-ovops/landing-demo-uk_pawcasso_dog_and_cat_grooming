# Pawcasso Concept Demo Brief

Audience: Glasgow dog and cat owners deciding whether to make an appointment. First-screen job: establish Pawcasso as a real Anniesland salon, show its dual-species focus and make calling clear.

Scope: a finished local static one-page concept. Sections: shopfront Hero, service ledger, appointment approach, grooming visuals, public review proof, address and Maps route. Primary CTA: `Call Pawcasso`. Secondary contact: email and Google Maps. No form, booking calendar, payment, login, CMS or availability claim.

Facts used: official logo, shopfront, two official grooming images, Glasgow address, telephone, email, appointment-only model, named service list and eight review excerpts user supplied from the official Pawcasso website. Customer authorization remains not verified. The cat visual is an internally logged concept image and is not presented as Pawcasso work or premises.

Design Read: Glasgow pet grooming - recognisable neighbourhood salon, not a generic pet landing page - register=brand/editorial - SPECTACLE=4 - hero-engine=one restrained image reveal and scroll-entry system.

## Skill receipt

- `website-delivery-sop`: retained the L0 local static boundary, scope/non-scope and evidence separation.
- `finesse-brief`: this brief defines the commercial first-screen task, section hierarchy, facts, CTA and non-scope for a commercial page rather than a workbench.
- `finesse-ui`: changed the substrate to an Anniesland place-led blue/white system, made the actual shopfront the single Hero asset and diversified the service ledger, image sequence, review rail and directions panel.
- `design-taste-frontend`: rejected generic pastel-pet cards, repeated balanced sections and blue-glow decoration. It drove the asymmetric work grid, mixed-height photographic rhythm, single accent and explicit 390px collapse.
- `media-use`: official assets remain frozen locally with matching SHA-256. The cat image remains an internally logged illustrative concept visual.
- `motion-design`: chose the calm motion personality: 580ms decelerating scroll entries, a 1s Hero photo settle, 180ms CTA feedback, and a slow review rail that pauses on hover. Each has a `prefers-reduced-motion` static fallback.
